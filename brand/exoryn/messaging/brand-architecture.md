# EXORYN Brand Architecture

## Masterbrand
**EXORYN**

Use the masterbrand for company, website, legal/commercial presence and top-level product identity.

## Product naming — recommended
These names are provisional and should not be independently trademarked or launched until product scope is frozen.

- **EXORYN Edge** — customer-controlled enforcement runtime.
- **EXORYN Signal** — fleet intelligence and behavioral analytics.
- **EXORYN Lab** — Digital Twin, replay and stress testing.
- **EXORYN Console** — operator interface.

Use functional descriptors in technical documentation until these product names are formally approved.

## Repository naming
Technical repository names may remain descriptive internally. The commercial product should surface EXORYN.
