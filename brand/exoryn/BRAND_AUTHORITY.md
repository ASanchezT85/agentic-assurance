## Authority

This directory is the source of truth for EXORYN's commercial visual identity.

The assets and rules stored here are version-controlled brand artifacts and must not be reinterpreted, regenerated, redrawn or replaced by implementation agents without explicit approval.

## Rules

1. EXORYN is the commercial masterbrand.
2. Brand assets are version-controlled artifacts.
3. SVG masters must not be regenerated by implementation agents.
4. Product code should consume approved brand tokens rather than invent parallel palettes.
5. The visual identity may not drift because of implementation convenience.
6. Material changes to logo, palette, typography, messaging or naming require explicit approval.
7. 3D objects and futuristic brand devices are not the corporate logo.
8. Internal repository and service names do not automatically become commercial product names.
9. AI image generation must not be used to redraw or replace canonical logo assets.
10. PNG exports are derivatives; SVG files remain authoritative.
11. The brand book controls logo use, color, typography, voice and visual language.
12. Product integration must occur in a separate authorized phase.
