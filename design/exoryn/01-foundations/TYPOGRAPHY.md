# Typography

## Product families

- **Space Grotesk** - display/headings/product navigation emphasis.
- **Inter** - body, controls, dense data UI.
- **IBM Plex Mono** - event names, IDs, hashes, policy/grant identifiers, timestamps where useful.

## Scale

| Token | Size | Line height | Use |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| display-lg | 40 | 48 | product/marketing bridge, rare in Console |
| heading-xl | 30 | 38 | surface title |
| heading-lg | 24 | 32 | major region |
| heading-md | 18 | 26 | card/panel title |
| body-lg | 16 | 24 | explanatory copy |
| body-md | 14 | 21 | default UI |
| body-sm | 12 | 18 | metadata |
| mono-sm | 12 | 18 | event IDs/hashes |
| metric-lg | 30 | 34 | primary KPI |

Use tabular figures for metrics. Never recreate the EXORYN wordmark by typing it.
