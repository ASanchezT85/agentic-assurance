## Product rule
Brand hues are retained, but UI text variants may be darker derivatives to meet WCAG contrast. This does not redefine the master brand palette; it defines accessible product usage.

## Semantic usage

- Electric Blue: active navigation, inspect/navigation actions, selected state.
- Signal Cyan: verified/live/connected accents; never generic decoration.
- Success: confirmed healthy/completed state, not “profitable”.
- Warning: degraded/stale/partial/unavailable caution.
- Danger: critical incident/refusal/destructive risk; never used merely because a trade is SELL.
- Purple derivative: declared/unverified provenance where a distinct axis is required.
- Neutral Slate: unknown, expired, cancelled, informational secondary state.

## Never
- green = buy;
- red = sell;
- cyan everywhere to imply “AI”;
- color-only status communication.
