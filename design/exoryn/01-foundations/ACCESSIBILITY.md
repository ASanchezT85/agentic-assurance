# Accessibility

Target: WCAG 2.2 AA.

- Minimum normal text contrast 4.5:1.
- Status always includes text/icon/shape, never color alone.
- Keyboard access for navigation, tables, filters, drawers and graph-node inspection.
- Visible 2 px focus ring with sufficient contrast.
- Touch targets >= 44 x 44 px on mobile.
- Charts require accessible summary/data-table alternatives.
- Do not place critical coverage/provenance only in hover tooltips.
- `Unavailable`, `Empty`, `Stale`, and `Error` states require explicit headings and explanatory copy.
- Respect reduced motion.
