# Product Design Principles

## P1 - Evidence before decoration
The interface exists to make decisions reconstructable. Visual polish must increase comprehension, not obscure evidence.

## P2 - Show truth state, not dashboard confidence
A missing source, an empty result, stale data, declared provenance and verified provenance are different facts and must look different.

## P3 - No single magic score
EXORYN shows the Fleet Risk Vector and coverage components. It does not manufacture a composite “AI risk” score unless a future calibrated model is explicitly authorized.

## P4 - Recommendation is not enforcement
A system recommendation is intelligence. An applied control is customer-authorized enforcement. They receive different labels, icons, hierarchy and copy.

## P5 - Institutional density
EXORYN is an operator product. Dense information is acceptable when hierarchy, scanning, keyboard navigation and progressive disclosure are strong.

## P6 - Read-only must feel intentional
The Console is not “missing buttons.” It is an observational control plane. Inspect, filter, search, compare, copy IDs and navigate are allowed. Mutating financial authority is not.

## P7 - Provenance travels with the metric
Coverage/provenance cannot live only in a tooltip. High directional imbalance over weak coverage must not look equivalent to the same metric over strong coverage.

## P8 - Time is part of truth
Every operational view must expose freshness or observation window where relevant. “Live” is a claim and must be earned.

## P9 - Corrections preserve history
Corrected evidence is visually linked; the original remains inspectable.

## P10 - One system, platform-specific workflows
Web, mobile and desktop share tokens and semantics, not necessarily identical layouts. Mobile is a monitoring companion; desktop can be a multi-pane workstation.
